Our Story

Last August 15, 2026, after two years of being together, Bram decided to pop the question — and Dee happily said yes.

To our friends and loved ones,

We're about to enter an exciting new chapter together — and we will do so with much gratitude for the love, laughter, and memories we've shared with you that brought us to this point in our lives. Our union, with the blessing of our families, will surely be made more meaningful in the presence (in person or spirit) of the people and relationships that we hold dear.

Our love story may have started with just the two of us, but it has been shaped by so many of you along the way. You've celebrated our happiest days, held space for us through the difficult ones, and reminded us, in countless little ways, what it means to be loved and surrounded by good people.

This little corner of the internet is our way of sharing the details and moments leading up to our wedding. We hope you enjoy exploring it as much as we've enjoyed putting it together.

Thank you for being part of our story, for cheering us on, and for loving us through all the seasons of our lives. We can't wait to celebrate with you!

- Bram and Dee
